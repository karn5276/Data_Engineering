no of numfiles = no of partition
eg: we partition based on country and in our dataset we have 24 unique country then total 
numFiles will be created equal to 24.


Partition on multiple column will multiple the numFiles
partitionBy(country)  === numFiles = 24
partitionBy(country,role)  == 24*7 = 168  # if distinct role = 7
